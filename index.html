PRIVACY & WELLBEING

Take back control of your data.

Practical steps for privacy, safer accounts and healthier screen time.

🔐

Reduce data sharing

  1. Review Accounts Center → Your information and permissions.
  2. Review Activity Off-Meta Technologies and disconnect unwanted businesses.
  3. Turn off contact syncing if not required; review previously uploaded contacts.
  4. Review ad preferences and available personalization controls.
  5. Remove unused connected apps and websites.
⏳

Reduce screen time

  1. Use Instagram's Time Spent controls, reminders and Quiet/Sleep mode where available.
  2. Set app limits through iOS Screen Time or Android Digital Wellbeing.
  3. Disable distracting notifications.
  4. Schedule specific times to check messages.
  5. Review habits rather than assuming FIYAGRAM can calculate exact screen time from ZIP logs.
🛡️

Keep your account safer

  1. Enable two-factor authentication, preferably using an authenticator app.
  2. Use a unique password and review active login sessions.
  3. Remove unknown devices and suspicious connected apps.
  4. Check privacy settings, tagged posts and message requests.
  5. Store exported ZIP files securely; delete unnecessary copies.
🗃️

Keep exported data private

  1. Analyze on a trusted device.
  2. Do not upload private archives to random sites.
  3. Keep extracted media and reports in protected folders.
  4. Use encrypted storage for backups.
  5. Close this page to discard its in-memory analysis.
